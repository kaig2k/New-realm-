**📜 ELDMERE UPDATE: Endgame, Pets & More** (part 1/4)

**🕳️ The Endless Abyss**
A new portal in the Nexus east wing (or `/abyss`) takes level 20 heroes down, floor after floor.
• Every floor is harder than the last: tougher monsters, better loot
• After each floor, pick 1 of 3 **boons** that last for your whole run
• Every 5th floor the **Abyssal Herald** waits at the bottom with 3 new uniques
• Weekly leaderboard for each class. Each class's #1 earns **Abyss Champion**
• Titles for clearing floors 10 and 25

**⚔️ Realm Siege**
Every 2 hours, **Vorgath the Siege Warlord** attacks the Godlands with his army.
• 15 minutes to bring him down, or he leaves
• He gets stronger the more players fight him
• Live panel showing his HP, time left, the top 3 damage dealers and your own share
• Loot depends on how much damage you did, and the **MVP** gets an extra roll
• 3 new Starforged uniques. Titles: **Siegebreaker** (MVP) and **Realm Defender**

**🏰 Guild Territory**
Four **Spires** stand at the edge of the Godlands. Stand by one with your guild to capture it.
• 60s alone, 20s with 3+ members. A single defender stops the capture
• Each Spire your guild holds = **+5 loot luck** for every member
• The guild that holds the Spires longest each week becomes **Lord of the Godlands** (+500 guild fame)
• `/spires` shows who holds what

**📜 ELDMERE UPDATE** (part 2/4)

**✨ Ascension**
A level 20 hero with all 11 stats maxed can **Ascend** (`/ascend`, or the button in the Skill Tree).
• Back to level 1, keeping your gear
• Earn a point in the **Ascendant Tree**: bonus XP, loot luck, gold, pet XP, HP and Attack for **every** hero on your account
• Each ascension gives your hero a brighter aura (bronze, silver, gold, then prismatic)

**👁️ The Oracle** (`O` or `/oracle`)
• Tracks every boss you've killed, how many times, and everything they dropped
• **Collections**: find every unique from a boss to give every hero on your account +1 to a stat and earn a title
• Titles for 5 collections and for completing them all

**🌳 Skill Tree Rework**
• 5 constellations: **Might, Guard, Fortune, Arcana, Hunt**
• 35 skills across 4 tiers. Deeper skills cost more points
• All skill points have been refunded so you can respec for free

**📜 ELDMERE UPDATE** (part 3/4)

**🐾 Pets Overhaul**
• Pets now **level up from the XP you earn** in realms. No more paying to feed them
• Pets **evolve**: Hatchling, Juvenile, Adult, Ascended
• At Adult, each species picks one of **two paths**, each with its own look (12 new pet forms)
• Pet skills: Fetch, Split, Night Sight, Fire Breath, Mana Spring, Stone Guard
• Evolved pets grow bigger and glow. Ascended pets get a halo
• **Pet bags**: Adults carry 2 items, Ascended carry 4 (`J`)
• **Pet Stable**: keep up to 6 extra pets
• **Evolution Stones** drop from bosses

**💀 Cursed Gear**
Bosses can now drop cursed top-tier gear with two boosted stats and one stat **below zero**.
Six curses: Bloodbound, Hexed, Feral, Gilded, Reckless, Brittle. High risk, high reward.

**🔮 Runes & Fortune's Anvil**
• **Runes** (from bosses) keep a stat locked when you reroll
• **Fortune's Anvil** at the Starforge: gamble an item a tier up... or down... or shatter it

**🌟 Godly & Primordial Drops**
Big full-screen reveals with animated metal titles: white gold for Godly, molten lava for Primordial. The whole server hears about it.

**📜 ELDMERE UPDATE** (part 4/4)

**🛒 Marketplace**
Brand-new full-size window: search, filters (kind, class, price), sorting, a scrolling item grid, a selling page and My Listings.

**🔧 Quality of Life**
• **Gear lock** (right-click or Ctrl+click): locked items can't be sold, listed, traded or dropped
• **Chat scrolling** with the mouse wheel or Page Up/Down
• **New keybinds**: `X` interact, `V` realm, `H` Godlands, `O` Oracle, `J` pet

• **Loot filter** (Settings)
• **Trade confirm** screen showing both sides
• Poison (Fan of Knives, acid flask) hits much harder and ignores Defense
• One realm open at a time for now, so everyone plays together
• `/bug what happened` sends a bug report straight to the staff

**👑 Roles**
• New **Owner**, **Moderator** and **VIP** roles with chat badges
• **VIP perks**: `/l20`, `/backpack` and +15 loot luck. Ask staff about VIP!

**🛡️ Anti-Cheat**
The anti-cheat no longer disconnects anyone. It quietly flags suspicious play for staff to review, so legit players won't get kicked anymore.
