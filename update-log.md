**📜 ELDMERE UPDATE: Boss Balance Pass**

Every boss in the game was fought by bots, 5 times each: bots that dodge like a good player, and bots that just stand there and shoot. The goal: **every boss can be dodged, and every boss punishes you if you don't.** Now a skilled player can beat nearly every boss without dying, and standing still gets you killed almost everywhere.

**🎯 Fairer, still deadly** (attacks now have gaps you can find, and more warning)
• **Astraeon**: his star spirals were a solid wall in the last phases. They now have lanes to dodge through, and his supernova throws fewer shards
• **Vorgath the Siege Warlord**: mace swings have gaps between the crescents, fewer soldiers join the fight, and his last stand fires a readable spiral
• **Archon Vesper, Tempestus, Nerezza, Vael'thrax**: their final-phase spirals are less dense and a little slower
• **Malgoroth**: his fan of shots spreads wider, and he no longer runs you down
• **The Skull Shrine**: its last-phase spirals and fire walls have room to breathe
• **Ignivar and Glacius**: the Shattered Sanctum's wardens are no longer deadlier than the boss they guard
• **Kael'zar, Morwyn and the Shattered Seraph**: blasts give you more time to leave, walls have wider gaps

**😵 Confused works like Realm of the Mad God**
• Instead of reversing your movement, Confused now swaps your keys around: **W moves you right, D moves you up, S moves you left, A moves you down**. It's the same every time, so you can learn to play through it

**⚔️ More of a fight**
• **Ssythra**, **Sylith the Frost Wyrm**, **the Tempest Seraph** and **the Tide Hermit** hit harder and have new attacks. You can't just stand there any more
• **Glacius** and **Morwyn** each have a new slow ring attack to read
• **Raid objectives**: the Blood Altars and Lightning Rods are tougher and fight back with a new attack each, so breaking them is a real stage, not a speed bump

**📜 ELDMERE UPDATE: Endgame, Pets & More** (part 1/5)

**🕳️ The Endless Abyss**
A new portal in the Nexus east wing (or `/abyss`) leads level 20 heroes to a **floating arena in the void**, where the waves never stop.
• Waves get much harder fast, with elites everywhere later on and more monsters for bigger groups
• **Wave modifiers**: Swarm, Elite Pack, Frenzy, Juggernauts, Bloodthirst, Splitters, Reinforcements, Ambush (up to 3 at once!)
• Take longer than 40s on a wave and **the Abyss grows restless**, sending elites until you finish it
• A boss every 5th wave, the **Abyssal Herald** every 10th, and **two bosses at once** from wave 20
• Monsters drop **nothing**. Instead, every 5 waves an **Abyssal Chest** appears, and the further you get, the better it is (up to **The Abyss's Hoard** at wave 30+, the only source of the 3 Abyss uniques)
• Pick a **boon** after each boss wave. Weekly leaderboard for each class: the #1s earn **Abyss Champion**

**⚔️ Realm Siege**
Every 2 hours, **Vorgath the Siege Warlord** marches on the Godlands. And he's HUGE now.
• Brand-new giant boss with his own animations: horned helm, molten armour, a siege hammer that cracks the ground when it lands
• His **war camp** rises from the Godlands around him (palisade, lava trenches, tents, siege ballistas) and the land burns
• The sky over the whole realm turns blood red while he's there
• 5 phases: the March, Siege Engines, the Charge, the Camp Burns, the Last Stand
• Break the ballistas to bring the palisade down, survive the overflowing trenches, then the whole camp catches fire
• 15 minutes to bring him down. Loot depends on your damage share, and the **MVP** gets an extra roll
• 3 Starforged uniques. Titles: **Siegebreaker** (MVP) and **Realm Defender**

**✨ Ascension**
A level 20 hero with all 11 stats maxed can **Ascend** (`/ascend`, or the button in the Skill Tree).
• Back to level 1, keeping your gear
• Earn a point in the **Ascendant Tree**: bonus XP, loot luck, gold, pet XP, HP and Attack for **every** hero on your account
• Each ascension gives your hero a brighter aura (bronze, silver, gold, then prismatic)

**👁️ The Oracle** (`O` or `/oracle`)

• **Collections**: find every unique from a boss to give every hero on your account +1 to a stat and earn a title
• Titles for 5 collections and for completing them all

**🌳 Skill Tree Rework**
• 5 constellations: **Might, Guard, Fortune, Arcana, Hunt**
